Environmental sources

Existing native sources in the current view. Overrides apply to the selected terrain variant; gameplay stays unchanged.

Object lighting follows each object's position, consistently across its sprite parts. Dimming can affect scenery, the player and enemies independently. Unchecked override controls keep inherited behavior.

Drag along the visible rock edge. Up to 16 points per stroke, within 512px. Explicit points let you avoid soft background details. Check tile depth and collision before tracing.

Sizes are radii in game pixels. Travel uses pixels per cycle: negative Y rises, positive Y falls, zero Y stays within the area. Spread 0 forms a column; 1 fills the width. Seed fixes the pattern when scrubbing. Changing area size keeps particle size fixed.

Ground mist searches down through the area to the first supported floor in each column. Mist height controls its depth above that floor. Free mist is airborne.

In Edit emitters, click an area to select it; drag its centre or body to move it; drag a corner to resize. Alt snaps to 16 pixels. Esc returns to tile selection.

Drag a rectangle up to 512 × 512 pixels on the map. Cyan edges support mist; green cells are solid; amber cells are partial collision. Scenery painting does not change collision. Erase removes intersecting authored mist areas; undo restores them.
