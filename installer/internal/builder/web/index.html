Your instruction manual, plus practical guidance for the workshop.

How do workshop projects and installed packs differ?

Opening a pack makes an editable workshop copy. Saving keeps your work, but does not update the game. Install your saved pack from the editor, restart the game, then select its package name in the game’s localization settings. Translated packs use enhanced text rendering.

The workshop ships no instruction manual. Install your own PDF and it is read here and in the game, from the Manual entry in the game’s settings.

About ActRaiser

Action & Simulation

Pulse-stopping action sequences combined with a Simulation Mode that lets you forge a new civilization — a game built to use the Super NES to the full. Long ago you and your people built a peaceful land; since then the evil Tanzra and his Guardians have made it a breeding ground for monsters. Punish Tanzra and give your people back the world they knew.

Box artwork and text © 1990–1992 Quintet / Enix, shown for identification. This project ships no game code, game data or instruction manual — the game is generated on this machine from your own ROM.
